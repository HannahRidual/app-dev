# My Favorite Movie

# Four Sisters and a Wedding

**Genre:** Drama/Comedy

### About the Movie
Four Sisters and a Wedding is a Filipino movie about four sisters who reunite to stop their younger brother from getting married. As they spend time together, they face family problems, misunderstandings, and personal struggles.

### Why I like it
I like Four Sisters and a Wedding because it shows the importance of family, love, and understanding. The movie is funny and emotional, and it teaches us that even though families have misunderstandings, they should always support and care for one another.

### My Favorite Characters
die, Bobbie, Alex, and Gabbie – I like the four sisters because they have different personalities, but they all show how much family matters.
